**## Chosen Approach**  
The most effective way to obtain a 1‑bit BitNet LLM is to perform knowledge distillation from a GPT‑OSS teacher while simultaneously training the student with binary‑weight quantization using a straight‑through estimator (STE) and progressive quantization curricula. First, generate a large pseudo‑labeled dataset of teacher logits for diverse prompts; then train the BitNet student on a combined loss of KL‑divergence to the teacher and token‑level cross‑entropy, applying STE to update the binary weights and leveraging BitNet’s lookup‑table kernels for efficient binary matrix multiplication. This pipeline follows the proven “BitNet Distillation” recipe that achieves near‑teacher performance with 1‑bit weights and yields up to nine‑fold inference speedups on GPUs [corpus] [web:https://arxiv.org/html/2510.13998v1] [github:https://github.com/microsoft/BitNet].
